# Hotel Aurora

Hotel Aurora is a 42-room independent hotel on the Lisbon coast, with one
restaurant (The Aurora Grill) and a small spa. This is the one property
description this repo uses everywhere - see docs/how-it-works.md "Design
decisions" 8 for why that matters.

## Property facts

- 42 rooms across four room types: Classic, Sea View, Junior Suite, Aurora Suite.
- Restaurant: The Aurora Grill, 60 covers, open for breakfast, lunch and dinner.
- Spa: three treatment rooms, open 09:00-19:00.
- Address: 1 Example Street, 1000-001 Lisbon, Portugal.
- Currency: EUR. Timezone: Europe/Lisbon.

## Rooms

- Classic (18 rooms): city-facing, 22 sqm.
- Sea View (14 rooms): partial or full sea view, 24 sqm.
- Junior Suite (7 rooms): separate seating area, 32 sqm.
- Aurora Suite (3 rooms): top-floor, private terrace, 45 sqm.

## The Aurora Grill

- Signature dish: the Aurora Burger, EUR 24.
- Also known for the Pan-Seared Salmon and a short, seasonal wine list.
- Competitors watched monthly: Bay View Bistro, Harbourside Grill, The Coastal Table.

## Policies

- Free cancellation up to 48 hours before arrival.
- Check-in from 15:00, check-out by 11:00.
- Pets allowed in ground-floor Classic rooms only, EUR 15/night.
